Nexa AI Capital operates from Malta, one of Europe's fastest-growing AI and fintech jurisdictions, combining frontier AI research with world-class regulatory infrastructure and a thriving tech ecosystem.

🏛️ Regulatory Leadership

EU-regulated jurisdiction with MiFID II compliance, pioneering crypto/blockchain frameworks, and a sandbox-first approach that accelerates AI-native fintech innovation while maintaining institutional-grade oversight.

💻 AI & Tech Talent Pool

Malta hosts a vibrant community of AI researchers, quants, and engineers. Home to leading tech firms and a growing number of AI labs, attracting talent from across Europe and globally.

🎰 Gaming & Fintech Legacy

Malta's established iGaming infrastructure (NetBet, other major operators) has built deep expertise in real-time trading systems, compliance automation, and secure financial rails—core competencies for AI investment platforms.

Strategic EU location with passporting rights, enabling seamless access to institutional LPs across Europe, Middle East, and Asia. Hub for global fund management and capital formation.
